# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Hackathon judges watching a live demo on the venue projector (MLK Pauley East Ballroom), and the operator persona the demo
plays: someone deciding where hostile drones came from and whether to send a survey drone. They read the map from across
a room, in short glances, while someone narrates.

## Product Purpose
Decision support on simulated sensor data: fuse noisy detections into drone tracks, estimate launch origins, link moving
launch teams, forecast the next launch, show which friendly routes are exposed, and let a named human approve a
surveillance-only survey drone. Success: in 90 seconds a judge sees drones appear, understands where they came from, and
sees a human approve every action.

## Brand Personality
Tactical, precise, sober. A telemetry terminal, not a video game. Calm under load; the drones are the loudest thing on
screen.

## Anti-references
- Weapons, targeting, engagement or strike imagery of any kind (crosshairs only as map reticles for estimated sites).
- Video-game HUDs: neon glow, glossy gradients, lens flares.
- SaaS dashboards: rounded cards, purple gradients, hero metrics.
- Anything that hides that the data is simulated.

## Design Principles
1. The drones are the protagonists: movement, heading and identity readable from the back of the room.
2. Every number says where it came from and how sure it is.
3. A human decides: approvals and recommendations always read as recommendations.
4. Simulated, always visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Projector-readable: high contrast on a dark substrate, nothing important below 13px, no meaning carried by color alone
(shape and text labels back every color). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (rotor spin, radar sweep and interpolated
motion stop).
